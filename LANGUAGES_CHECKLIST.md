# **Languages for Dataset Collection**

We aim to collect datasets for **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** and other tasks across the world’s languages. Below is a list of languages we are currently considering. Contributors are encouraged to:  
1. Verify the list and ensure coverage for these languages.  
2. Add any missing languages under the **"Missing Languages"** section.

3. **Update the "Missing Languages"** section above with your additions.
