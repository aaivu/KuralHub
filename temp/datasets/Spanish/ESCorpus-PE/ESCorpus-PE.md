# ESCorpus-PE (Peruvian Spanish Emotional Speech Corpus)  

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** ESCorpus-PE  
- **Institution:** Universidad Nacional de San Agustín de Arequipa  
- **Year:** 2021  
- **Authors:** Alessandra, C.Q., et al.  
- **Homepage:** [Zenodo Repository](https://zenodo.org/records/5793223)  

## Dataset Content

- **Duration:** Not specified  
- **Sessions:** Multiple recording sources  
- **Speakers:** 80 (44 male, 36 female)  
- **Utterances:** 3,749 total recordings  
- **Language:** Spanish (Peruvian)  
- **Sources:** Interviews, TV reports, political debates, testimonials  

## Technical Details

- **Format:** Audio recordings  
- **Source:** YouTube videos  
- **Size:** 1.9 GB (Corpus_Globalv1)  
- **Data Types:** Natural speech recordings  

## Emotional Content

- **Dimensional Annotations:**  
  - Valence
  - Arousal
  - Dominance

- **Annotation Details:**
  - Continuous emotional dimensions
  - Natural emotional expressions
  - Context-preserved emotions
