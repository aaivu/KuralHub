# MESD (Mexican Emotional Speech Database)  

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** Mexican Emotional Speech Database (MESD)  
- **Institution:** Multiple Mexican Institutions  
- **Year:** 2023  
- **Authors:** Velázquez-Morales, P., et al.  
- **Homepage:** [Mendeley Data](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/cy34mh68j9/5)  

## Dataset Content

- **Duration:** Single-word utterances  
- **Sessions:** Multiple recording sessions  
- **Speakers:** 11 total non-professional actors
  - 3 female adults (mean age 23.33 ± 1.53)
  - 2 male adults (mean age 24 ± 1.41)
  - 6 children (mean age 9.83 ± 1.17)
- **Words:** Multiple nouns and adjectives  
- **Utterances:** 24 per emotion per voice type  
- **Language:** Mexican Spanish  

## Technical Details

- **Format:** WAV audio files  
- **Audio:** 48 kHz sampling rate  
- **Bit Depth:** 24 bits per sample  
- **Recording:** Professional setup
  - Microphone: Sennheiser e835 (100 Hz - 10 kHz)
  - Interface: Focusrite Scarlett 2i4
  - Software: REAPER
- **Data Types:** Single-word utterances  
- **Size:** 96.7 MB  

## Emotional Content

- **Primary Emotions:**  
  - Anger  
  - Disgust  
  - Fear  
  - Happiness  
  - Neutral  
  - Sadness  

- **Special Versions:**
  - Natural recordings
  - Two naturalness-reduced versions (female voices, corpus B)

## Corpus Organization

- **Corpus A:**
  - Nouns and adjectives repeated across emotions
  - Same words for all voice types
  - Natural recordings only

- **Corpus B:**
  - Words controlled for:
    * Age-of-acquisition
    * Frequency of use
    * Familiarity
    * Concreteness
    * Valence
    * Arousal
  - Includes naturalness variations for female voices
