# IndoWaveSentiment (Indonesian Emotional Speech Dataset)

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** IndoWaveSentiment  
- **Institution:** Not specified  
- **Year:** 2023  
- **Authors:** Iqbal, M., Sari, D. K., Siahaan, E.  
- **Homepage:** [IndoWaveSentiment Dataset](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/j9ytfdzy27/1)  

## Dataset Content

- **Duration:** Not specified  
- **Sessions:** Single recording session  
- **Speakers:** 10 actors (5 male, 5 female)  
- **Sentences:** Same sentence repeated 3 times  
- **Utterances:** 300 total recordings  
- **Language:** Indonesian  

## Technical Details

- **Format:** WAV files  
- **Audio:** 16 kHz sampling rate  
- **Bit Depth:** 32-bit  
- **Recording:** Mono channel cardioid vocal microphone  
- **Equipment:** Microphone positioned within 10 cm from speakers  
- **Data Types:** Audio recordings  

## Emotional Content

- **Primary Emotions:**
  - Neutral  
  - Happy  
  - Surprised  
  - Disgusted  
  - Disappointed  

- **Secondary Emotions:** None specified
