# Danish Emotional Speech Database (DES)

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: Danish Emotional Speech Database (DES)
- **Institution**: Center for Person Kommunication, Aalborg University
- **Year**: 1997
- **Authors**: I. F. Engberg and A.V. Hansen
- **Homepage**: [ELRA Catalogue](http://universal.elra.info/product_info.php?products_id=78)
- **Project**: Part of VAESS (Voice Attitudes and Emotions in Speech Synthesis)

## Dataset Content
- **Total Samples**: 260 recordings
- **Speakers**: 4 professional actors (2 male, 2 female)
- **Speech Types**:
  - 2 words ("yes" and "no")
  - 9 sentences (including 4 questions)
  - 2 passages of fluent speech

## Technical Details
- **Language**: Danish
- **Type**: Studio speech recordings
- **Format**: DAT tape recordings
- **Sampling Rate**: 48 kHz
- **Recording Setup**:
  - High-quality microphone
  - 5 Hz high-pass filter
  - Acoustically damped studio
- **Annotation**: Phonetic transcription by T. Brondsted

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Angry
  - Happy
  - Sad
  - Surprise
  - Neutral

## Research Impact
- **License**: Available through ELRA
- **Applications**: 
  - Speech Emotion Recognition (SER)
  - Speech Synthesis with Emotions
  - Assistive technology development
- **Purpose**: Development of emotional speech synthesis for communication aids

## Validation Process
- **Validators**: 20 native Danish speakers
- **Age Range**: 18-58 years
- **Method**: Forced-choice listening test
- **Recognition Rate**: 67% average accuracy
