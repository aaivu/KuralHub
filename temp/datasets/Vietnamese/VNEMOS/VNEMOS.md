# VNEMOS (Vietnamese Speech Emotion Dataset)

## Dataset Overview

**Name:** Vietnamese Speech Emotion Dataset (VNEMOS)  
**Institution:** Not specified in source  
**Year:** 2023-2024  
**Homepage:** bit.ly/VNEMOS  
**IEEE Paper:** https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10616411

## Dataset Content

**Duration:** Approximately 30 minutes of audiovisual data  
**Segments:** 250 total segments (equally divided among emotions)  
**Sources:** 27 (movies, movie series and live shows)  
**Data Types:**  
- Natural (from conversations and live TV shows)
- Acted (from movies and live shows)

## Technical Details

**Audio:** Speech audio in Vietnamese language  
**Video:** Segments from movies and live shows  
**Data Types:** Speech audio from various sources  
**Performance:** 89% accuracy in evaluation

## Emotional Content

**Primary Emotions** (equally distributed):
- Anger
- Happiness
- Sadness
- Neutral
- Anxiety/Fear
