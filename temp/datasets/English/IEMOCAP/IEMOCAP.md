# IEMOCAP (Interactive Emotional Dyadic Motion Capture Database)

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: Interactive Emotional Dyadic Motion Capture (IEMOCAP) Database
- **Institution**: USC SAIL Laboratory
- **Year**: 2008
- **Authors**: Busso, C., Bulut, M., Lee, C.C., et al.
- **Homepage**: [https://sail.usc.edu/iemocap/](https://sail.usc.edu/iemocap/)

## Dataset Content
- **Duration**: 12 hours of audiovisual data
- **Sessions**: 5 dyadic sessions
- **Speakers**: 10 actors (5 male, 5 female)
- **Dialogues**: 151 (302 individual videos)
- **Utterances**: 10,039 total
  - 5,255 scripted
  - 4,784 spontaneous

## Technical Details
- **Motion Capture**: VICON system (8 cameras, 120 fps)
- **Audio**: WAV format (48kHz)
- **Video**: High-resolution digital
- **Markers**: 53 facial markers + head/hand tracking
- **Data Types**: Audio, video, motion capture, transcriptions

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Anger
  - Happiness
  - Sadness
  - Frustration
  - Neutral

- **Secondary Emotions**:
  - Disgust
  - Fear
  - Excitement
  - Surprise
