# RAVDESS (Ryerson Audio-Visual Database of Emotional Speech and Song)

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: Ryerson Audio-Visual Database of Emotional Speech and Song (RAVDESS)
- **Institution**: Ryerson University (now Toronto Metropolitan University)
- **Year**: 2018
- **Authors**: Steven R. Livingstone & Frank A. Russo
- **Homepage**: [https://zenodo.org/records/1188976](https://zenodo.org/records/1188976)

## Dataset Content
- **Total Files**: 7,356 files
- **Total Size**: 24.8 GB
- **Actors**: 24 professional actors
  - 12 female
  - 12 male
- **Vocalizations**: 104 unique vocalizations per actor
  - 60 spoken utterances
  - 44 sung utterances

## Technical Details
- **Audio Format**: WAV (16-bit, 48kHz)
- **Video Format**: HD video with facial tracking
- **Language**: North American English
- **Statements**: Two lexically-matched statements
- **Duration**: ~3 seconds per file

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Calm
  - Happy
  - Sad
  - Angry
  - Fearful
  - Surprise
  - Disgust
  - Neutral

## Expression Intensity
- **Intensity Levels**:
  - Normal
  - Strong

## Research Impact
- **Published**: PLOS ONE (2018)
- **DOI**: [10.1371/journal.pone.0196391](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0196391)
- **Widely used**: Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) research
- **Validation**: Independently validated by 247 raters
