# Emozionalmente (Italian Crowdsourced Emotional Speech Dataset)  

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** Emozionalmente  
- **Institution:** Politecnico di Milano  
- **Year:** 2022  
- **Authors:** Catania, F.  
- **Homepage:** [Zenodo Repository](https://zenodo.org/records/6569824)  

## Dataset Content

- **Duration:** ~7.3 hours (3.81 seconds × 6,902 samples)  
- **Sessions:** Multiple recording sessions  
- **Speakers:** 431 amateur actors  
- **Sentences:** 18 different sentences  
- **Utterances:** 6,902 total recordings  
- **Language:** Italian  

## Technical Details

- **Format:** WAV audio files  
- **Audio:** 16 kHz sampling rate  
- **Bit Depth:** 16 bits per sample  
- **Recording:** Non-professional equipment  
- **Data Types:** Mono-channel audio recordings  
- **Size:** 581.4 MB (compressed)  

## Emotional Content

- **Primary Emotions:**  
  - Anger  
  - Disgust  
  - Fear  
  - Joy  
  - Neutral  
  - Sad  
  - Surprise  

- **Secondary Emotions:** None specified
