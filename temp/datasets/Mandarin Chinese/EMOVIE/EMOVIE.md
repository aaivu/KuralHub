# EMOVIE (Mandarin Emotional Movie Speech Dataset)  

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** EMOVIE Dataset  
- **Institution:** Not specified  
- **Year:** 2021  
- **Authors:** Cui, C., et al.  
- **Homepage:** [EMOVIE Project Page](https://viem-ccy.github.io/EMOVIE/dataset_release.html)  

## Dataset Content

- **Duration:** 4.18 hours total  
- **Sources:** Seven Mandarin movies (feature films and comedies)  
- **Utterances:** 9,724 total samples  
  - Training: 9,524 samples
  - Validation: 100 samples
  - Testing: 100 samples
- **Language:** Mandarin Chinese  

## Technical Details

- **Format:** WAV audio files  
- **Audio:** 22.05 kHz sampling rate  
- **Source:** Front Center channel of 5.1/7.1 movie audio  
- **Extraction:** ffmpeg tool for audio processing  
- **Average Length:** 1.78 seconds per utterance  
- **Text Length:** 8.93 characters average  

## Emotional Content

- **Emotion Polarity Levels:**  
  - Strong Negative (-1): 179 samples
  - Slight Negative (-0.5): 4,573 samples
  - Neutral (0): 1,783 samples
  - Slight Positive (0.5): 3,171 samples
  - Strong Positive (1): 78 samples

- **Annotation Details:**
  - Human-labeled annotations
  - 5-point polarity scale
  - Includes text transcriptions
