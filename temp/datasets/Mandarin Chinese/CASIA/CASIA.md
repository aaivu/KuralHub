# CASIA (Chinese Academy of Sciences Emotional Speech Corpus)  

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** CASIA Chinese Emotion Corpus  
- **Institution:** Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences  
- **Year:** 2005  
- **Authors:** Zhang, S., et al.  
- **Homepage:** [Kaggle Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/xiangminjie/casia-ser)  

## Dataset Content

- **Duration:** Not specified  
- **Sessions:** Single recording session  
- **Speakers:** 4 professional speakers (2 male, 2 female)  
- **Sentences:** Multiple Mandarin utterances  
- **Utterances:** 7,200 total recordings on Kaggle  
  - 1,200 files per emotion category
  - Balanced across emotions
- **Language:** Mandarin Chinese  

## Technical Details

- **Format:** PCM WAV audio files  
- **Audio:** 16 kHz sampling rate  
- **Bit Depth:** 16 bits per sample  
- **Recording:** Noise-free environment  
- **Signal Quality:** ~35 dB SNR  
- **Size:** 476.13 MB  
- **License:** Apache 2.0  

## Emotional Content

- **Primary Emotions:**  
  - Anger (1,200 files)  
  - Fear (1,200 files)  
  - Happy (1,200 files)  
  - Neutral (1,200 files)  
  - Sad (1,200 files)  
  - Surprise (1,200 files)
