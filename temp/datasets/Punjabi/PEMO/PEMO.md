# Punjabi Emotional Speech Database (PEMO)

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: PEMO (Punjabi Emotional Speech Database)
- **Institution**: 
  - Punjabi University, Patiala Punjab
  - Multani Mal Modi College, Patiala Punjab
- **Year**: 2022
- **Authors**: 
  - C. Singla
  - S. Singh
- **Homepage**: [IJRITCC Article](https://ijritcc.org/index.php/ijritcc/article/view/5734)

## Dataset Content
- **Total Duration**: 12 hours 35 minutes
- **Total Utterances**: 22,000
- **Speakers**: 60 native Punjabi speakers
  - Age range: 20-45 years
- **Speech Types**:
  - Natural utterances from movies
  - Web series content

## Technical Details
- **Language**: Punjabi
- **Type**: Speech recordings
- **Format**: WAV files
  - Bit depth: 16 bits
  - Sampling rate: 44.1 KHz
  - Channels: Mono
- **Processing Tool**: PRAAT software
- **Source**: Online Punjabi movies and web series

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Anger
  - Sad
  - Happy
  - Neutral

## Research Impact
- **Applications**: 
  - Speech Emotion Recognition (SER)
  - Human and computer-based emotion evaluation
  - Punjabi language processing research
- **Usage**: Research in emotion recognition systems

## Validation Process
- **Annotators**: Three experts
- **Method**: 
  - Consensus-based annotation
  - All annotators must agree on label
- **Qualifications**: 
  - Thorough knowledge of Punjabi language
  - Expertise in emotion recognition
