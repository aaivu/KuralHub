# OGVC (Online Gaming Voice Chat Corpus)  

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** Online Gaming Voice Chat Corpus with Emotional Label (OGVC)  
- **Institution:** National Institute of Informatics (NII)  
- **Year:** 2014  
- **Authors:** Arimoto, Y., Kawatsu, H.  
- **Homepage:** [SRC Repository](https://research.nii.ac.jp/src/en/OGVC.html)  

## Dataset Content

- **Natural Speech:**
  - 13 speakers (9 male, 4 female)
  - 6 dialogues (5 dyadic, 1 triad)
  - 9,114 utterances
- **Acted Speech:**
  - 4 professional actors (2 male, 2 female)
  - 17 dialogue sequences
  - 2,656 utterances
- **Language:** Japanese  

## Technical Details

- **Natural Speech Format:**
  - WAV audio files  
  - 48 kHz sampling rate  
  - 16 bits per sample  
  - Mono channel
- **Acted Speech Format:**
  - WAV audio files
  - 44.1 kHz sampling rate
  - 16 bits per sample
  - Mono channel
- **Recording:** Laboratory office setting with head-set microphone  
- **Distribution:** 2 DVDs  

## Emotional Content

- **Annotation Model:**
  - Plutchik's three-dimensional circumplex model
  - Four intensity levels:
    * Neutral (0)
    * Weak (1)
    * Medium (2)
    * Strong (3)
  - Applied to both natural and acted speech
