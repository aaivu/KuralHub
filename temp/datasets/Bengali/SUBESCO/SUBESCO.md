# SUBESCO (SUST Bangla Emotional Speech Corpus)

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** SUST Bangla Emotional Speech Corpus (SUBESCO)  
- **Institution:** Shahjalal University of Science and Technology  
- **Year:** 2021  
- **Authors:** Sultana, S., Rahman, M. S., Selim, M. R., Iqbal, M. Z.  
- **Homepage:** [SUBESCO on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sushmit0109/subescobangla-speech-emotion-dataset)  

## Dataset Content

- **Duration:** 7 hours 40 minutes 40 seconds  
- **Sessions:** 7,000 recordings total  
- **Speakers:** 20 actors (10 male, 10 female)  
- **Age Range:** 21–35 years  
- **Sentences:** 10 phonetically balanced sentences  
- **Utterances:** 7,000 total recordings  

## Technical Details

- **Format:** 32-bit mono WAV files  
- **Audio:** 48 kHz sampling rate  
- **Duration:** 4 seconds per recording  
- **Recording:** Professional studio quality  
- **Data Types:** Audio recordings  

## Emotional Content

- **Primary Emotions**
  - Anger  
  - Disgust  
  - Fear  
  - Happiness  
  - Neutral  
  - Sadness  
  - Surprise  

- **Secondary Emotions**

None specified
