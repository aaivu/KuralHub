# BAVED (Basic Arabic Vocal Emotions Dataset)

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** Basic Arabic Vocal Emotions Dataset (BAVED)  
- **Institution:** Not specified  
- **Year:** 2021  
- **Authors:** Ali AOUF  
- **Homepage:** [BAVED GitHub](https://github.com/40uf411/Basic-Arabic-Vocal-Emotions-Dataset)  

## Dataset Content

- **Duration:** 1,935 audio recordings  
- **Sessions:** Multiple recording sessions  
- **Speakers:** 61 actors (45 male, 16 female)  
- **Age Range:** 18–23 years  
- **Words:** 7 Arabic words  
- **Size:** 97.8 MB  

## Technical Details

- **Format:** Audio WAV files  
- **Audio:** 16 kHz sample rate (downsampled from 48 kHz)  
- **Channels:** Mono (1 channel)  
- **Bitrate:** 256 kbit/s  
- **Data Types:** Audio recordings  

## Emotional Content

- **Primary Emotions:**
  - **Level 0:** Low emotion/tired  
  - **Level 1:** Neutral/standard  
  - **Level 2:** High emotion (positive/negative)
