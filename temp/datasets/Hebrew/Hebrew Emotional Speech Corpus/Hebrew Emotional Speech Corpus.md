# Hebrew Emotional Speech Corpus

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: Hebrew Emotional Speech Corpus
- **Institution**: 
  - Holon Academic Institute of Technology, Israel
  - Tel Aviv-Brull Community Mental Health Center
  - Sackler Faculty of Medicine, Tel-Aviv University
  - Yale Child Study Center
- **Year**: 2000
- **Authors**: Noam Amir, Samuel Ron, Nathaniel Laor
- **Homepage**: [ISCA Archive Paper](https://www.isca-archive.org/speechemotion_2000/amir00_speechemotion.pdf)

## Dataset Content
- **Total Duration**: 186 minutes
- **Speakers**: 31 total (16 male, 15 female)
  - Initially 40 speakers, reduced to 31 after validation
- **Speech Types**:
  - Spontaneous recalled emotional events
  - Natural emotional expressions
- **Duration per Recording**: ~1 minute per emotion per speaker
- **Rest Period**: 120-240 seconds between trials

## Technical Details
- **Language**: Hebrew
- **Type**: Speech recordings
- **Analysis Parameters**:
  - 30ms analysis windows
  - 10ms window overlap
  - F0 detection using autocorrelation
  - Order 3 median post-filtering
- **Feature Extraction**:
  - Pitch statistics
  - Intensity measurements
  - Jitter and shimmer analysis
  - Speech rate metrics

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Anger
  - Fear
  - Joy
  - Sadness
  - Disgust
  - Neutral state
