# MediaTeam Speech Corpus

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: MediaTeam Speech Corpus
- **Institution**: University of Oulu, MediaTeam Research Group
- **Year**: 2003
- **Authors**: Tapio Seppänen, Juhani Toivanen, et al.
- **Homepage**: [University of Oulu](https://www.oulu.fi/en)

## Dataset Content
- **Total Duration**: Not specified
- **Speakers**: 14 professional actors
  - 8 male actors
  - 6 female actors
  - Age range: 25-50 years
- **Speech Types**:
  - ~100-word passage per emotion
  - Two emotional dialogues

## Technical Details
- **Language**: Finnish
- **Type**: Speech recordings
- **Format**: WAV files
- **Recording Specifications**:
  - 48 kHz sampling rate
  - 16-bit depth
- **Recording Environment**: Unechoic studio
- **Recording Equipment**: DAT (Digital Audio Tape)

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Neutral
  - Happiness/Joy
  - Sadness
  - Fear
  - Anger
  - Boredom
  - Disgust
