# emoUERJ (Brazilian Portuguese Emotional Speech Database)  

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** emoUERJ  
- **Institution:** Universidade do Estado do Rio de Janeiro  
- **Year:** 2021  
- **Authors:** Pinto Gomes Junior  
- **Homepage:** [Zenodo Repository](https://zenodo.org/records/5427549)  

## Dataset Content

- **Duration:** Not specified  
- **Sessions:** Single recording session  
- **Speakers:** 8 actors (4 male, 4 female)  
- **Sentences:** 10 predefined sentences  
- **Utterances:** 377 total recordings  
- **Language:** Brazilian Portuguese  

## Technical Details

- **Format:** Audio recordings  
- **Size:** 105.1 MB (compressed)  
- **Recording:** Professional actors  
- **Data Types:** Acted speech recordings  
- **File Naming:** [gender][speaker_id][emotion][recording_id]
  - gender: 'm' (man) or 'w' (woman)
  - speaker_id: 01-04
  - emotion: h/a/s/n
  - recording_id: unique identifier

## Emotional Content

- **Primary Emotions:**  
  - Happiness (91 samples)  
  - Anger (94 samples)  
  - Sadness (100 samples)  
  - Neutral (92 samples)  

- **Secondary Emotions:** None specified
