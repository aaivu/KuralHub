Benchmark results

Portuguese is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 11 runs the mean test accuracy was 60.0% (median 63.0%). The best run reached 91.0% with whisper-small. Compare with other languages.

The VERBO database is a speech emotion dataset designed to evaluate the effectiveness of Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) systems. It comprises recordings of 14 sentences spoken by 12 professional speakers, including six actors and six actresses. Each speaker expresses six basic emotions—happiness, sadness, anger, fear, disgust, and surprise—along with a neutral state. In total, the dataset contains 1,176 audio recordings. A statistical analysis was conducted to assess how well these recordings represent emotional speech, making the VERBO database a valuable resource for training and evaluating SER models.

The dataset was developed at the State University of Rio de Janeiro to address the challenge of language specificity in Speech Emotion Recognition (SER), particularly for Portuguese, where existing databases are limited. It consists of 377 audio recordings, each featuring an actor expressing one of four emotions: happiness (91 samples), anger (94 samples), sadness (100 samples), and neutrality (92 samples). Each file is named using a structured format that encodes the actor's gender, ID, expressed emotion, and recording number. For example, "w04a11" represents the eleventh recording of actress 04 portraying anger. This dataset aims to facilitate the development of SER models tailored to the Portuguese language.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID emoUERJ). See the results above.
