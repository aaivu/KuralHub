Benchmark results

Korean is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 11 runs the mean test accuracy was 57.7% (median 70.0%). The best run reached 81.0% with whisper-small. Compare with other languages.

The Korean Emotional Speech Dataset (KESDy18) was collected in 2018 for speech emotion recognition research. It contains recordings of 30 voice actors uttering 20 sentences in four emotions (“angry,” “happy,” “neutral,” “sad”). Six annotators labeled each segment with one of seven categorical emotions, arousal (1–5), and valence (1–5). Labels were determined by majority vote and averaged scores. The dataset includes recordings from two microphones: a cellphone’s built-in mic (PM) and an external mic (EM). The published version contains KESDy18_EM (Shure S35) data.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID KESDy18). See the results above.
