Benchmark results

Russian is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 11 runs the mean test accuracy was 34.5% (median 40.0%). The best run reached 51.0% with hubert-base-ls960. Compare with other languages.

Dusha is a bi-modal corpus suitable for speech emotion recognition (SER) tasks. The dataset consists of about 300 000 audio recordings with Russian speech, their transcripts and emotional labels. The corpus contains approximately 350 hours of data. Four basic emotions that usually appear in a dialog with a virtual assistant were selected: Happiness (Positive), Sadness, Anger and Neutral emotion.

The dataset focuses on emotional speech dialogues in Russian, capturing live speech performed by actors who express specific emotions in pre-scripted dialogues. The dataset contains approximately 3.5 hours of speech, with each dialogue lasting around 3 minutes. It includes audio samples recorded at either 16,000 Hz or 44,100 Hz, corresponding to one of seven labeled emotional states: anger, disgust, fear, enthusiasm, happiness, neutral, and sadness. In addition to the emotional labels, each sample also includes speech-to-text transcriptions of the spoken dialogue. The dataset is useful for tasks related to Speech Emotion Recognition (SER), emotion detection in speech, and speech-to-text modeling.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID RESD). See the results above.
