Benchmark results

Hindi is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 11 runs the mean test accuracy was 48.8% (median 61.0%). The best run reached 71.0% with whisper-small. Compare with other languages.

The Emotional Hindi Speech Corpus developed at IPEC includes recordings from 28 speakers (14 male, 14 female) aged 20–30, expressing six emotions across 12 sentences. It is designed for applications in speech recognition, synthesis, speaker identification, and emotion analysis. The dataset also examines the impact of emotions on F0 contour and spectrum.

The dataset created by Koolagudi et al. in 2011 is an emotional speech corpus recorded in Hindi, featuring 10 speakers (5 male and 5 female) within the age ranges of 28–48 years for males and 20–30 years for females. Each speaker expressed 8 different emotions by speaking 15 sentences in a single session, with a total of 10 sessions conducted for corpus collection. The dataset comprises 12,000 utterances, amounting to 9 hours of recordings. The number of syllables per sentence ranges from 9 to 17, while the word count varies between 4 and 7. This dataset is valuable for emotional speech analysis and natural language processing applications.
