Benchmark results

Turkish is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 11 runs the mean test accuracy was 54.9% (median 48.0%). The best run reached 84.0% with whisper-small. Compare with other languages.

The dataset consists of 5304 speech signals from 55 Turkish movies, with their textual contents extracted. These speech signals are labeled both categorically (happy, surprised, sad, angry, fear, neutral, and other) and in a 3-dimensional emotional space (valence, activation, and dominance). It is designed for emotion recognition and acoustic analysis of Turkish emotional speech.

The Turkish Emotion-Voice Database (TurEV-DB) is a corpus of over 1735 tokens, consisting of 82 words uttered by human subjects across four emotions: angry, calm, happy, and sad. The speech data were produced by amateur actors, processed through a denoising procedure, and checked by assessors. The database includes the speech corpus, spectrograms, extracted prosodic features, prosodic data graphs, and model activations. It also supports machine learning experiments using CNN and SVM models, with frequency-filtered speech signals across three frequency ranges (0-8000 Hz, 0-5000 Hz, 500-8000 Hz) for improved emotion detection.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID TurEV-DB). See the results above.
