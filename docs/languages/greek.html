Benchmark results

Greek is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 11 runs the mean test accuracy was 46.6% (median 54.0%). The best run reached 75.0% with hubert-base-ls960. Compare with other languages.

AESDD v1.0 was created on October 2017 in the Laboratory of Electronic Media, School of Journalism and Mass Communications, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, for the needs of Speech Emotion Recognition research of the Multidisciplinary Media & Mediated Communication Research Group (M3C, http://m3c.web.auth.gr/). It is a collection of utterances of emotional speech acted by professional actors. This version is the initial state of AESDD. The purpose of this project the continuous growth of the database through the collaborative effort of the M3C research group and theatrical teams.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID AESDD). See the results above.

The GreThE (Greek Theatrical Emotion Dataset) is a collection of 500 audio samples from 90 unique speakers across 23 Greek theatrical plays. The dataset is annotated for valence(negative/neutral/positive) and arousal (weak/neutral/strong). It is designed for Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) tasks, particularly in the context of theatrical performances. The dataset includes Mel-spectrograms and segment feature vectors for each audio sample, making it suitable for machine learning and deep learning experiments.

The SEWA dataset is a multimodal dataset designed for emotion recognition and analysis. It includes audio and video data collected from participants during natural interactions. The dataset aims to study emotional states in real-world scenarios, focusing on cross-cultural differences and spontaneous emotional expressions. Data was collected using wearable devices and audio-visual recordings, making it a rich resource for affective computing research.
