Benchmark results

Indonesian is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 11 runs the mean test accuracy was 72.1% (median 77.0%). The best run reached 99.0% with hubert-base-ls960. Compare with other languages.

The dataset consists of audio recordings extracted from the Indonesian widescreen movie Cek Toko Sebelah. It was chosen for its well-structured dialogues, high audio quality, and minimal background noise. The dataset is designed to limit variations in speakers, accents, and recording techniques by using a single movie, ensuring consistency. This approach is similar to past studies that controlled variations by using a limited number of speakers and sentences per emotion class. However, restricting the dataset to one film also limits the total amount of data collected.

IndoWaveSentiment is an Indonesian speech dataset for emotion classification, featuring 300 mono-channel 16 kHz audio recordings from 10 actors (5 male, 5 female). Each actor repeated a sentence three times across five emotions: neutral, happy, surprised, disgusted, and disappointed. Designed for deep learning and signal processing, it supports research in sentiment analysis and voice emotion classification.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID IndoWaveSentiment). See the results above.
