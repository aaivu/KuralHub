Benchmark results

German is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 11 runs the mean test accuracy was 55.5% (median 53.0%). The best run reached 86.0% with whisper-large. Compare with other languages.

The EMODB database is a freely available German emotional speech dataset created by the Institute of Communication Science at the Technical University of Berlin, Germany. It contains 535 utterances recorded by 10 professional speakers (5 males and 5 females), representing seven emotions: anger, boredom, anxiety, happiness, sadness, disgust, and neutral. The recordings were made at a 48-kHz sampling rate and later down-sampled to 16 kHz. This dataset is widely used for emotion recognition tasks in speech processing.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID EmoDB). See the results above.
