Benchmark results

Tamil is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 11 runs the mean test accuracy was 30.6% (median 29.0%). The best run reached 44.0% with whisper-small. Compare with other languages.

Collected by the Emota team, this dataset contributes to Tamil Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) focusing on Sri Lankan Tamil. It includes recordings from undergraduates who took Drama Grade B or above at the O/L level, covering diverse regions within Sri Lanka.

Note: Please fill out the request form they provide to sign the dataset agreement, after which you can obtain the dataset from the team.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID EmoTa). See the results above.
