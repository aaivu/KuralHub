Paper
Design and Implementation of Czech Database of Speech Emotions

The Czech Database of Speech Emotions was developed to support research in speech emotion recognition for the Czech language. It comprises short audio recordings extracted from broadcasts of three radio stations and one television channel. The dataset covers four emotional states: joy, anger, sadness, and neutral. The recordings were manually selected to ensure clear representation of each emotion.

The Slavic Call Center Emotional Speech Corpus is designed for automatic recognition of emotional expressions from spontaneous Slavic speech. The corpus is built from speech extracts obtained from real call center recordings involving four Slavic languages: Czech, Polish, Russian, and Slovak. It encompasses five different emotional states: anger, happiness, sadness, surprise, and neutral. The dataset captures the natural emotional expressions found in customer service interactions, providing valuable data for research in emotion recognition systems.
