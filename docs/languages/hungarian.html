Benchmark results

Hungarian is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 11 runs the mean test accuracy was 36.7% (median 43.0%). The best run reached 52.0% with hubert-base-ls960. Compare with other languages.

This dataset comprises recordings of three Hungarian sentences, each articulated to express eight fundamental emotions: happiness, sadness, anger, fear, surprise, disgust, contempt, and neutral. These recordings were performed by nonprofessional speakers. The primary objective of this dataset is to facilitate the examination of human emotion perception and to support the development of automatic emotion recognition systems. To achieve this, the dataset was utilized in perception tests comparing human recognition of emotions and in training Hidden Markov Models (HMM) for automatic emotion recognition, optimizing parameters such as acoustic preprocessing and the number of states in the models.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID HungarianEmotionalSpeechCorpus). See the results above.

The SEWA dataset is a multimodal dataset designed for emotion recognition and analysis. It includes audio and video data collected from participants during natural interactions. The dataset aims to study emotional states in real-world scenarios, focusing on cross-cultural differences and spontaneous emotional expressions. Data was collected using wearable devices and audio-visual recordings, making it a rich resource for affective computing research.
