Low-dimensional feature space derivation for emotion recognition

The Polish Emotional Database consists of emotionally expressive speech recordings in Polish. It contains utterances spoken by professional actors who were instructed to express specific emotions. The database was created for research on emotion recognition systems and feature space derivation for affective speech processing. Each utterance was recorded to represent one of six emotional states: neutral, joy, anger, sadness, fear, and boredom.

Note: The dataset is not publicly available. You may need to contact the authors for access.

The Polish Multimodal Database of Emotional Speech, Video and Gestures is a comprehensive collection of emotional expressions in three modalities: facial expressions, body movements/gestures, and speech. Created by Sapinski et al. (2019), this dataset features recordings of 16 professional actors (8 males and 8 females, aged 25-64) from Teatr Nowy performing various emotional expressions.

The data was collected in a controlled environment - a quiet, well-lit theater rehearsal room with a green background. For facial expression recording, medium shots were used to keep the actor's face in frame while allowing for natural movement during emotional expressions. For body movement capture using Kinect, the recording frame included the entire body down to the legs.

This multimodal approach allows researchers to study emotional expressions across different communication channels simultaneously, making it a valuable resource for multimodal emotion recognition research, affective computing, and human-computer interaction studies focused on the Polish language.

nEMO: Dataset of Emotional Speech in Polish
Download
GitHub Repository, HuggingFace

The nEMO dataset is a Polish speech emotion corpus designed for Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) research. The corpus contains over 3 hours of emotional speech recordings performed by 9 professional actors. The dataset covers six basic emotions: anger, fear, happiness, sadness, surprise, and neutral.

The recordings are high-quality audio files suitable for developing and testing emotion recognition algorithms. The dataset was created by a Polish research team and has been made available for research purposes under a Creative Commons license.

Note: Use according to CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license terms.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID nEMO). See the results above.
