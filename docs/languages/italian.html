Benchmark results

Italian is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 22 runs the mean test accuracy was 50.4% (median 55.5%). The best run reached 80.0% with whisper-large. Compare with other languages.

DEMoS (Database of Elicited Mood in Speech) is an Italian emotional speech corpus containing 9,365 emotional and 332 neutral samples from 68 native speakers (23 females, 45 males) across seven emotions: anger, sadness, happiness, fear, surprise, disgust, and guilt. Unlike acted speech, DEMoS features emotion elicited through Mood Induction Procedures (MIP). A subset of 1,564 prototypical samples from 59 speakers was selected using self- and external assessment. The dataset has been published in Language Resources and Evaluation.

EMOVO is the first Italian emotional speech corpus, featuring recordings from 6 actors simulating the Big Six emotions (disgust, fear, anger, joy, surprise, sadness) plus neutrality across 14 sentences. The recordings were captured using professional equipment in the Fondazione Ugo Bordoni laboratories. A validation test with 48 listeners achieved an overall recognition accuracy of 80%, with anger, sadness, and neutrality being the most easily recognized emotions, while joy and disgust were more challenging to identify.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID EMOVO). See the results above.

Emozionalmente is an Italian speech emotion dataset containing 6,902 labeled audio samples from 431 amateur actors expressing the Big Six emotions (anger, disgust, fear, joy, sadness, surprise) plus neutrality. The recordings are 16-bit mono .wav files at 16 kHz, averaging 3.81 seconds. Emotional labels represent the actors' intended emotions, validated by 829 human annotators with 66% accuracy. The dataset includes audio files and three CSVs detailing actor demographics, evaluator data, and emotion labels.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID Emozionalmente). See the results above.
