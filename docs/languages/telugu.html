Telugu is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. The IITKGP Simulated Emotion Speech Corpus (IITKGP-SESC) is a Telugu speech emotion dataset recorded using professional artists from All India Radio (AIR), Vijayawada, India. It contains emotionally simulated speech covering eight emotions: Happiness, Surprise, Anger, Disgust, Sadness, Fear, Sarcasm, and Neutral, derived from neutral statements. The dataset is designed for emotion analysis in speech, enabling the development of emotion-specific models using features from the vocal tract system, excitation source, and prosody. The quality of emotions is validated through subjective listening tests, and statistical models are used for classification.

The Telugu Emotion Speech dataset is a collection of 468 audio samples in the Telugu language, designed for speech emotion recognition. It includes five emotion categories: Happy, Sad, Angry, Neutral, and Surprised. This dataset is valuable for emotion analysis in Telugu speech and can be used for training and evaluating machine learning models in affective computing.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID Telugu-Dataset). See the results above.
