IIITSaint-EmoMDB: Carefully Curated Malayalam Speech Corpus with Emotion and Self-Reported Depression Ratings
Contact
Authors

IIITSaint-EmoMDB is a meticulously curated Malayalam speech corpus designed to explore the relationship between emotional perception and mental health conditions, such as depression and anxiety. The dataset includes speech samples annotated with emotion ratings on valence and arousal scales, alongside self-reported depression ratings. This resource aims to facilitate research in understanding the intricate connections between emotions and mental health.

Malayalam Kannur Dialect Speech Emotion Recognition using Machine Learning
Contact
mail

The KMSER Dataset was created to address the lack of speech emotion recognition resources for the Malayalam language, specifically the Kannur dialect. Audio samples were collected from freely accessible YouTube videos, WhatsApp chats, and self-recorded files. The dataset focuses on four primary emotions: happy, angry, sad, and neutral. Feature extraction techniques such as Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficient (MFCC), chroma shift, spectral centroid, spectral bandwidth, rolloff, zero crossing rate, and mel spectrogram were employed. Classification was performed using Support Vector Machine (SVM) and Random Forest algorithms. (thegrenze.com)

Contact
Dr. Firoze A, Dr. Rajiv A, Dr. Bantop

This dataset is part of research focused on emotion recognition from speech using artificial neural networks. It includes gender-dependent databases and utilizes features like discrete wavelet transforms, low-pass filters, and frequency filtering to extract speech features for emotion classification. The dataset is part of an effort to advance affective computing and automatic emotion recognition.
