Benchmark results

Persian is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 11 runs the mean test accuracy was 67.1% (median 76.0%). The best run reached 83.0% with whisper-small. Compare with other languages.

ShEMO is a large-scale Persian emotional speech database containing 3,000 semi-natural utterances (3 hours 25 minutes) from 87 native speakers. It includes six emotion categories: anger, fear, happiness, sadness, surprise, and neutral. The dataset was annotated by 12 raters with a 64% inter-annotator agreement and is available for academic research.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID SHEMO). See the results above.
