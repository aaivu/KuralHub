Benchmark results

Odia is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 11 runs the mean test accuracy was 64.4% (median 64.0%). The best run reached 83.0% with wavlm-large. Compare with other languages.

SITB-OSED is the first open-source large Odia speech emotion database developed by Silicon Institute of Technology, Bhubaneswar. The dataset contains recordings from 20 professional Odia native speakers covering five major Odia dialects (Cuttacki, Baleswari, Berhampuri, Sambalpuri, and Phulbani). Each utterance ranges from 3.5s to 8s and was recorded in a quiet environment. The dataset represents balanced coverage of six primary emotions across all major Odia dialects.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID SITB-OSED). See the results above.
