Benchmark results

Urdu is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 22 runs the mean test accuracy was 49.6% (median 45.5%). The best run reached 88.0% with wav2vec2-base. Compare with other languages.

The URDU dataset contains emotional utterances of Urdu speech gathered from Urdu talk shows. There are 400 utterances of four basic emotions in the book: Angry, Happy, Neutral, and Emotion. There are 38 speakers (27 male and 11 female). This data is created from YouTube. Speakers are randomly selected.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID Urdu-Dataset). See the results above.

The Urdu Speech Emotion Corpus (UrSEC) is a comprehensive dataset for Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) in Urdu, addressing the lack of large and diverse datasets in this language. It contains 3500 speech samples from 10 professional actors, representing seven emotional states: Angry, Fear, Boredom, Disgust, Happy, Neutral, and Sad. The samples are sourced from Pakistani Urdu drama serials and telefilms, ensuring natural and diverse speech. Each emotional class has 500 samples, with detailed metadata for easy organization and use in research. UrSEC is rigorously validated for accuracy and reliability, supporting advancements in Urdu SER.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID UrduSER). See the results above.
