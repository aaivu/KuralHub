Benchmark results

Amharic is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 11 runs the mean test accuracy was 63.6% (median 75.0%). The best run reached 90.0% with whisper-small. Compare with other languages.

The Amharic Speech Emotion Dataset (ASED) is a pioneering resource for Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) in the Amharic language. It encompasses recordings from 65 native speakers across four dialects: Gojjam, Wollo, Shewa, and Gonder. Participants delivered 2,474 audio samples, each lasting between 2 to 4 seconds, expressing five distinct emotions: neutral, fearful, happy, sad, and angry. The recordings underwent evaluation by eight judges, achieving a high agreement level (Fleiss kappa = 0.8). This dataset serves as a valuable benchmark for developing and testing SER models tailored to Amharic. (arxiv.org)

Note: The dataset is publicly available, so no request is needed.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID ASED). See the results above.
