Benchmark results

French is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 11 runs the mean test accuracy was 42.1% (median 38.0%). The best run reached 70.0% with wavlm-large. Compare with other languages.

The Canadian French Emotional (CaFE) speech dataset focuses on emotional speech in Canadian French, featuring six sentences spoken by 12 actors (6 male, 6 female) across six basic emotions plus neutral speech. Each emotion is expressed in two intensity levels (mild and strong). The dataset is available in high-resolution (192 kHz, 24-bit) and a downsampled version (48 kHz, 16-bit) under a Creative Commons (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) license.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID CaFE). See the results above.

The Oréau French emotional speech database was recorded in a quiet environment to support research on emotional speech and speech synthesis. It includes 79 classroom-relevant utterances covering 7 emotions, spoken by 32 non-professional speakers. Two versions exist: the first with 502 utterances, and the second with 434 utterances retained after a perception test confirmed 90% accuracy in emotion recognition and naturalness.
