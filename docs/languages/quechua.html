Benchmark results

Quechua is one of the 29 languages in the KuralHub benchmark. Each of the 11 frozen speech models was trained with a small classification head on the shared emotions and scored on a held-out test split. Over all 11 runs the mean test accuracy was 58.7% (median 71.0%). The best run reached 83.0% with whisper-large. Compare with other languages.

Quechua Collao Corpus

The dataset comprises recordings of words and sentences in the Quechua Collao language. These utterances were specifically collected to represent nine categorical emotions, aiming to facilitate research in automatic emotion recognition systems for this language. Quechua Collao corpus for automatic emotion recognition in speech. Audios are provided, alongside csv files with labels from 4 annotators for valence, arousal, and dominance values, using a 1 to 5 scale. Categorical labels are also included, as well as the script used for recording. This script contains sets of words and sentences written in Quechua Collao for 9 emotions.

Used in the KuralHub benchmark (run ID Quechua-Collao-Corpus). See the results above.
