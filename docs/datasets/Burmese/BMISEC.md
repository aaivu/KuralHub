## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

The Burmese Movies Interviews Speech Emotion Corpus (BMISEC) is a collection of natural speech segments extracted from Myanmar movies and interview recordings. The dataset consists of 1,332 utterances covering seven primary emotions. It is designed for Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) research and has been validated using DenseNet-Emotion.
