## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

This dataset is used for speech-based emotion classification in the Burmese language. It was introduced in the research conducted by Tin Lay Nwe, Foo Say Wei, and Liyanage C. De Silva, focusing on recognizing emotions from speech signals. The dataset is useful for Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) tasks.
