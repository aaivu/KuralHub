## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The EU-Emotion Voice Database is a comprehensive collection of emotional voice recordings designed for scientific research. It comprises 2,159 validated stimuli performed by 54 actors across multiple languages, including English, Swedish, and Hebrew. Each actor expressed 20 distinct emotional states plus a neutral state, resulting in a rich dataset for studying vocal emotion recognition and related fields.
