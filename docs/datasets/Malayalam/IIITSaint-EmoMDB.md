## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

IIITSaint-EmoMDB is a meticulously curated Malayalam speech corpus designed to explore the relationship between emotional perception and mental health conditions, such as depression and anxiety. The dataset includes speech samples annotated with emotion ratings on valence and arousal scales, alongside self-reported depression ratings. This resource aims to facilitate research in understanding the intricate connections between emotions and mental health.
