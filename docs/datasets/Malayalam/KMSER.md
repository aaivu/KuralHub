## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

The KMSER Dataset was created to address the lack of speech emotion recognition resources for the Malayalam language, specifically the Kannur dialect. Audio samples were collected from freely accessible YouTube videos, WhatsApp chats, and self-recorded files. The dataset focuses on four primary emotions: happy, angry, sad, and neutral. Feature extraction techniques such as Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficient (MFCC), chroma shift, spectral centroid, spectral bandwidth, rolloff, zero crossing rate, and mel spectrogram were employed. Classification was performed using Support Vector Machine (SVM) and Random Forest algorithms. ([thegrenze.com](https://thegrenze.com/pages/servej.php?association=GRENZE&fn=380.pdf&id=1958&issue=2&journal=GIJET&name=Malayalam+Kannur+Dialect+Speech+EmotionRecognition+using+Machine+Learning&volume=9&year=2023))
