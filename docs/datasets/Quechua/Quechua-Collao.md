# 🎙️ Quechua Collao Corpus - Quechua

🔹 **Dataset Name:** A speech corpus of Quechua Collao for automatic dimensional emotion recognition  <br/> 
🔹 **Language:** Quechua  <br/> 
🔹 **Dataset Type:** 🎙️ Audio <br/>
🔹 **Emotion Categories:** Happy, Sad, Bored, Fear, Sleepy, Calm, Excited, Angry, and Neutral  <br/> 
🔹 **Size:** Number of samples: 12420, speakers: 6, duration: 15 hours  <br/> 
🔹 **Recording Environment:** 🎭 Acted  <br/> 
🔹 **License:** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)  <br/> 
🔹 **Access:** 🔗 Open <br/> 

---

## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The dataset comprises recordings of words and sentences in the Quechua Collao language. These utterances were specifically collected to represent nine categorical emotions, aiming to facilitate research in automatic emotion recognition systems for this language.
Quechua Collao corpus for automatic emotion recognition in speech. Audios are provided, alongside csv files with labels from 4 annotators for valence, arousal, and dominance values, using a 1 to 5 scale.
Categorical labels are also included, as well as the script used for recording. This script contains sets of words and sentences written in Quechua Collao for 9 emotions.
