## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
EMOVO is the first Italian emotional speech corpus, featuring recordings from 6 actors simulating the Big Six emotions (disgust, fear, anger, joy, surprise, sadness) plus neutrality across 14 sentences. The recordings were captured using professional equipment in the Fondazione Ugo Bordoni laboratories. A validation test with 48 listeners achieved an overall recognition accuracy of 80%, with anger, sadness, and neutrality being the most easily recognized emotions, while joy and disgust were more challenging to identify.
