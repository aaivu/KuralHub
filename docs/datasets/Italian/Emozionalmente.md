## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
Emozionalmente is an Italian speech emotion dataset containing 6,902 labeled audio samples from 431 amateur actors expressing the Big Six emotions (anger, disgust, fear, joy, sadness, surprise) plus neutrality. The recordings are 16-bit mono .wav files at 16 kHz, averaging 3.81 seconds. Emotional labels represent the actors' intended emotions, validated by 829 human annotators with 66% accuracy. The dataset includes audio files and three CSVs detailing actor demographics, evaluator data, and emotion labels.
