## 📜 **Datase Overview**

The Romanian Language Sounds (SRoL) dataset is an online resource designed as a "dictionary of sounds and words" for the Romana language. It encompasses various linguistic elements, including vowels, consonants, diphthongs, and sentences articulated with distinct emotoal states. The emotional subset comprises recordings expressing happiness, sadness, fury, and a etral tone. Each sentence was recorded three times by speakers aged between 25-35 years from the central region of Moldova, ensuring consistencyad clarity. The dataset serves both educational and research purposes, facilitating studies in speech emotion recognition and linguisic analysis. <br/>
[Emotion recognition of the SROL Romanian database using fuzzy KNN algorithm](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/6408133)
