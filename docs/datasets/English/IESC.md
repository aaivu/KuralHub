## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The Indian Emotional Speech Corpora (IESC) is a dataset containing 600 emotional speech recordings from 8 North Indian speakers (5 males, 3 females). The audio files, recorded in a noise-free environment using a mobile phone and headset, cover five emotions: neutral, happy, angry, sad, and fearful. Each .wav file follows a structured naming convention indicating emotion, repetition, speaker, and sentence ID.
