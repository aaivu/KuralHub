## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The RAVDESS (Ryerson Audio-Visual Database of Emotional Speech and Song) is a dataset containing 1440 speech-only audio files (16-bit, 48kHz WAV) recorded by 24 professional actors (12 female, 12 male). The dataset includes seven emotions—calm, happy, sad, angry, fearful, surprise, and disgust—each performed at two intensity levels (normal and strong), along with a neutral expression. The recordings feature two lexically-matched statements in a neutral North American accent. The full dataset, including speech and song, is available on Zenodo (24.8 GB).
