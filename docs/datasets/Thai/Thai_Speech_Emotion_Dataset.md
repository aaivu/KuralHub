## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The THAI SER dataset comprises 27,854 utterances (41 hours, 36 minutes) across five emotions: Neutral, Anger, Happiness, Sadness, and Frustration. Collected from 200 actors (112 female, 88 male), the recordings are categorized into Studio (soundproof and non-soundproof environments) and Zoom sessions.
