## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

The CHEAVD dataset (Chinese Natural Emotional Audio-Visual Database) is a comprehensive collection of emotional expressions in Mandarin Chinese. It contains 2322 emotional segments extracted from Chinese movies, TV shows, and interviews, representing natural emotional expressions.

The dataset is annotated with 26 distinct emotion categories: neutral, angry, happy, sad, worried, anxious, disgust, surprise, blamed, sarcastic, aggrieved, curious, fearful, embarrassing, nervous, confused, proud, helpless, hesitant, contemptuous, frustrated, serious, anticipated, shy and guilty. Each segment is labeled with a primary emotion and potentially a secondary emotion label.

CHEAVD uniquely captures the complexity of human emotions by including:

- Non-prototypical emotions (such as shy, nervous, proud, frustrated)
- Multi-emotion labels where emotions co-occur (e.g., anger accompanied by disgust)
- Fake/suppressed emotions where internal and external expressions differ

The annotation was performed by four annotators with moderate consistency (kappa coefficient around 0.5), with neutral being the most represented emotion, reflecting real-world emotion distribution. The dataset includes demographic information about speakers, including gender and age range.
