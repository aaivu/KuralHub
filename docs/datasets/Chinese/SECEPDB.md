## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

The SECEPDB dataset addresses a significant limitation in emotion interaction studies by providing a specialized database focused on elderly individuals. Collected from 16 elderly actors (8 female and 8 male) performing in daily conversations from TV series, this multimodal database contains both audio and video recordings.

The database comprises 810 speech-video excerpts extracted from 118 conversations, covering seven distinct emotion categories: anger, boredom, happiness, sadness, surprise, neutrality, and disgust. To ensure quality and reliability, emotion annotations were evaluated by a panel of 18 participants (10 men and 8 women).

A unique feature of SECEPDB is its documentation of emotional transitions during conversations, making it particularly valuable for researchers studying the dynamics of emotional interactions in elderly populations. This resource contributes significantly to the development of more natural and responsive human-computer interaction systems tailored for older adults.
