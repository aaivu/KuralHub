# 🎙️ CASIA - Mandarin Chinese

🔹 **Dataset Name:** CASIA Chinese Emotion Corpus <br/>
🔹 **Language:** Mandarin Chinese <br/>
🔹 **Dataset Type:** 🎙️ Audio <br/>
🔹 **Emotion Categories:** Anger, Fear, Happy, Neutral, Sad, Surprise <br/>
🔹 **Size:** 7,200 recordings, 8 speakers (4 male, 4 female) <br/>
🔹 **Recording Environment:** 🎭 Acted <br/>
🔹 **License:** Apache 2.0 <br/>
🔹 **Access:** 🔗 Open <br/>

---

## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

The CASIA Chinese Emotion Corpus was developed by the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences. It contains emotional speech recordings from professional speakers expressing six different emotions in Mandarin Chinese. The recordings were conducted in a noise-free environment with approximately 35 dB SNR.
