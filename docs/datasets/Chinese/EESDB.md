## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

The Chinese Elderly Emotion Database (EESDB) is a specialized speech corpus focusing on emotional expressions of elderly Chinese speakers. The database consists of speech samples collected from Chinese television broadcasts, featuring 16 individuals (8 male and 8 female) all over 60 years of age. Each utterance is categorized into one of seven emotional states: anger, disgust, fear, happiness, neutral, sadness, and surprise. To ensure quality and reliability, the database underwent a rigorous perception test conducted by independent evaluators who were not involved in the voice collection or annotation process. Only utterances that achieved over 60% agreement on emotional labels were included in the final database, ensuring high consistency in emotional expression representation.
