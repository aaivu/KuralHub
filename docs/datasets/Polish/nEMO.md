## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

The nEMO dataset is a Polish speech emotion corpus designed for Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) research. The corpus contains over 3 hours of emotional speech recordings performed by 9 professional actors. The dataset covers six basic emotions: anger, fear, happiness, sadness, surprise, and neutral.

The recordings are high-quality audio files suitable for developing and testing emotion recognition algorithms. The dataset was created by a Polish research team and has been made available for research purposes under a Creative Commons license.
