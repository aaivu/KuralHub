## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

The Amharic Speech Emotion Dataset (ASED) is a pioneering resource for Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) in the Amharic language. It encompasses recordings from 65 native speakers across four dialects: Gojjam, Wollo, Shewa, and Gonder. Participants delivered 2,474 audio samples, each lasting between 2 to 4 seconds, expressing five distinct emotions: neutral, fearful, happy, sad, and angry. The recordings underwent evaluation by eight judges, achieving a high agreement level (Fleiss kappa = 0.8). This dataset serves as a valuable benchmark for developing and testing SER models tailored to Amharic. ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02710))
