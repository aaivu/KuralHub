## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

The Urdu-Sindhi Speech Emotion Corpus is a dataset collected at Mehran University of Engineering & Technology, Pakistan by a research team led by Dr. Zafi Sherhan Syed and Dr. Sajjad Ali Memon. The dataset consists of 701 audio recordings across seven emotions: anger, disgust, happiness, neutral, sarcasm, sadness, and surprise in two low-resource languages of South Asia - Urdu and Sindhi.

Due to ethical restrictions, the raw audio recordings are not publicly available. Instead, the researchers provide five feature sets extracted using the OpenSmile toolkit.

The research team offers to compute specific features locally for interested researchers and share those features upon request. For such requests, contact Dr. Zafi Syed at zafisherhan.shah@faculty.muet.edu.pk.
