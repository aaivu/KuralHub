## 📜 **DatasetOverview**

The Filipino Multimodal Emotion Database (FilMED) was developed to support the creation of affective systems for TALA, an ambient intelligent emahic space. The dataset comprises 11,430 audio-video clips featuring both acted and spontaneous emotional expressions from 2 subjects. Emotions are annotated using Filipino labels: kasiyahan (happiness), kalungkutan (sadness), galit (anger), takot (fear), gulat (surprise), and pandidii(disgust). Additionally, 20 coders provided valence and arousal annotations usigFeeltrace. The dataset has been utilized to develop automatic affect recogniion systems.
