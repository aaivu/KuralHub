## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The VERBO database is a speech emotion dataset designed to evaluate the effectiveness of Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) systems. It comprises recordings of 14 sentences spoken by 12 professional speakers, including six actors and six actresses. Each speaker expresses six basic emotions—happiness, sadness, anger, fear, disgust, and surprise—along with a neutral state. In total, the dataset contains 1,176 audio recordings. A statistical analysis was conducted to assess how well these recordings represent emotional speech, making the VERBO database a valuable resource for training and evaluating SER models.
