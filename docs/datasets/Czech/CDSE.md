## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The Czech Database of Speech Emotions was developed to support research in speech emotion recognition for the Czech language. 
It comprises short audio recordings extracted from broadcasts of three radio stations and one television channel. 
The dataset covers four emotional states: joy, anger, sadness, and neutral.
The recordings were manually selected to ensure clear representation of each emotion.
