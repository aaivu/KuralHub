# Kazakh-Russian Emotion Corpus

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: Kazakh-Russian Emotion Corpus
- **Institution**: Suleyman Demirel University, Kaskelen, Kazakhstan
- **Year**: 2020
- **Authors**: Kanat Kozhakhmet, Rakhima Zhumaliyeva, Aisultan Shoiynbek, Nazerke Sultanova
- **Homepage**: [Applied Mathematics & Information Sciences](https://digitalcommons.aaru.edu.jo/amis/vol14/iss1/8/)

## Dataset Content
- **Total Files**: 16,160 audio files
- **Total Duration**: 14+ hours
- **Actors**: 101 non-professional actors
  - 48 female (average age 20)
  - 53 male (average age 21)
- **Vocalizations**: 20 unique sentences per actor
  - 10 Kazakh sentences
  - 10 Russian sentences

## Technical Details
- **Audio Format**: WAV (16-bit, 16kHz)
- **Audio Type**: Mono
- **Languages**: Kazakh and Russian
- **Duration**: Variable length files

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Anger
  - Boredom
  - Disgust
  - Happiness
  - Sadness
  - Neutral
  - Fear

## Research Impact
- **Published**: Applied Mathematics & Information Sciences (2020)
- **DOI**: [10.18576/amis/140108](https://doi.org/10.18576/amis/140108)
- **Model Performance**: 82.07% accuracy in emotion recognition
- **Validation**: Validated by 5 focus groups (48 raters total)
