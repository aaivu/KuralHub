# ShEMO (Sharif Emotional Speech Database)  

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** Sharif Emotional Speech Database (ShEMO)  
- **Institution:** Sharif University of Technology  
- **Year:** 2019  
- **Authors:** Nezami, O.M., et al.  
- **Homepage:** [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/pariajm/sharif-emotional-speech-dataset)  

## Dataset Content

- **Duration:** 3 hours and 25 minutes  
- **Sessions:** Radio play recordings  
- **Speakers:** 87 native Persian speakers
  - 31 females
  - 56 males
- **Utterances:** 3,000 total recordings  
- **Language:** Persian  

## Technical Details

- **Format:** WAV files  
- **Audio:** 44.1 kHz sampling rate  
- **Bit Depth:** 16 bits per sample  
- **Channel:** Mono  
- **File Naming:** GXXEYY format
  - G: Gender (F/M)
  - XX: Speaker ID
  - E: Emotion (A/F/H/N/S/W)
  - YY: Utterance number

## Emotional Content

- **Primary Emotions (utterances):**  
  - Anger (1,059)  
  - Fear (38)  
  - Happiness (201)  
  - Sadness (449)  
  - Surprise (225)  
  - Neutral (1,028)
