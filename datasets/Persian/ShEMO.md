## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
ShEMO is a large-scale Persian emotional speech database containing 3,000 semi-natural utterances (3 hours 25 minutes) from 87 native speakers. It includes six emotion categories: anger, fear, happiness, sadness, surprise, and neutral. The dataset was annotated by 12 raters with a 64% inter-annotator agreement and is available for academic research.
