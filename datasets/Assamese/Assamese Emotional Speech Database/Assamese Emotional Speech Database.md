# ASED (Assamese Emotional Speech Database)

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: Assamese Emotional Speech Database (ASED)
- **Institution**: Assam Don Bosco University, Guwahati, Assam, India
- **Year**: 2021
- **Authors**: Nupur Choudhury & Uzzal Sharma
- **Homepage**: Not publicly available

## Dataset Content
- **Total Files**: 1,750 utterances
- **Total Size**: Not specified
- **Actors**: 25 (15 female, 10 male)
  - Age range: 20-33 years (mean: 26 years)
  - Regional distribution: Upper Assam, Lower Assam, plain areas
- **Vocalizations**: 35 unique vocalizations per actor
  - 5 sentences per emotion
  - 2 tonal intensities per sentence

## Technical Details
- **Audio Format**: Not specified
- **Video Format**: Not available
- **Language**: Standard Assamese (dialect-free)
- **Statements**: Five emotionally-neutral statements
- **Duration**: 3-6 seconds per file

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Neutral
  - Happy
  - Sad
  - Fear
  - Disgust
  - Surprise
  - Angry

## Expression Intensity
- **Intensity Levels**:
  - Lower
  - Higher

## Research Impact
- **Published**: International Journal of Computer Sciences and Engineering (2021)
- **DOI**: Not specified
- **Application**: Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) research
- **Validation**: Independently validated by 10 raters
- **Performance**: 
  - MFCC features: Up to 94.4% accuracy
  - Energy features: Up to 88.5% accuracy
  - F0 features: Up to 84.2% accuracy
