# MELD (Multimodal EmotionLines Dataset)

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** Multimodal EmotionLines Dataset (MELD)  
- **Institution:** National University of Singapore  
- **Year:** 2019  
- **Authors:** Poria, S., Hazarika, D., Majumder, N., et al.  
- **Homepage:** [MELD Homepage](https://affective-meld.github.io/)  

## Dataset Content

- **Duration:** 13,708 utterances  
- **Sessions:** 1,433 dialogues  
- **Source:** Friends TV series  
- **Utterances:** 13,708 total utterances  
- **Base Dataset:** Enhanced version of EmotionLines  
- **Format:** Multi-party conversations  

## Technical Details

- **Format:** Multimodal data  
- **Audio:** Available for each utterance  
- **Video:** Visual features included  
- **Data Types:** Text, audio, and visual features  
- **Annotations:** Emotion and sentiment labels  

## Emotional Content

- **Primary Emotions**
  - Anger  
  - Disgust  
  - Sadness  
  - Joy  
  - Neutral  
  - Surprise  
  - Fear  

- **Secondary Labels**
- Sentiment (Positive)  
- Sentiment (Negative)  
- Sentiment (Neutral)  

## Research Impact

- **Citations:** Widely cited in emotion recognition research  
- **Influential Citations:** Used in multimodal conversation analysis  
- **Publication:** ACL 2019
