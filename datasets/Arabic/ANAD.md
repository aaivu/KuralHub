## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The Arabic Natural Audio Dataset (ANAD) was created to recognize emotions in speech, specifically targeting the detection of happiness, anger, and surprise in Arabic conversations. It aims to enhance communication for the deaf community by embedding emotion detection into speech recognition systems, improving their ability to interpret both speech and emotional context. The dataset was collected from eight videos of live calls from Arabic talk shows, with speech segments manually labeled by 18 listeners for emotional content. Each video was divided into 1-second speech units, resulting in a corpus of 1,384 records. Acoustic features, such as MFCC, intensity, and fundamental frequency, were extracted and analyzed using 19 statistical functions, totaling 950 features. This dataset is a step toward improving speech-to-text and text-to-speech systems for the hearing-impaired.
