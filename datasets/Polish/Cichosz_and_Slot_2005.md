## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

The Polish Emotional Database consists of emotionally expressive speech recordings in Polish. It contains utterances spoken by professional actors who were instructed to express specific emotions. The database was created for research on emotion recognition systems and feature space derivation for affective speech processing. Each utterance was recorded to represent one of six emotional states: neutral, joy, anger, sadness, fear, and boredom.
