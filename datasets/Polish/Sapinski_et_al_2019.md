## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

The Polish Multimodal Database of Emotional Speech, Video and Gestures is a comprehensive collection of emotional expressions in three modalities: facial expressions, body movements/gestures, and speech. Created by Sapinski et al. (2019), this dataset features recordings of 16 professional actors (8 males and 8 females, aged 25-64) from Teatr Nowy performing various emotional expressions.

The data was collected in a controlled environment - a quiet, well-lit theater rehearsal room with a green background. For facial expression recording, medium shots were used to keep the actor's face in frame while allowing for natural movement during emotional expressions. For body movement capture using Kinect, the recording frame included the entire body down to the legs.

This multimodal approach allows researchers to study emotional expressions across different communication channels simultaneously, making it a valuable resource for multimodal emotion recognition research, affective computing, and human-computer interaction studies focused on the Polish language.
