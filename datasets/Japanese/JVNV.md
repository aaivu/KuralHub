## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The JVNV dataset is a Japanese emotional speech corpus that uniquely integrates both verbal expressions and nonverbal vocalizations, such as laughter and sobbing, to capture a richer emotional spectrum. It contains 3.94 hours of high-quality speech recorded at 48 kHz in WAV format from four speakers (two male, two female), covering six emotions: anger, disgust, fear, happiness, sadness, and surprise. The dataset comprises 1,615 utterances, each expressing a specific emotion, with annotated time intervals for nonverbal sounds. JVNV also features two recording conditions: a regular session, where nonverbal phrases are predefined, and a phrase-free session, where speakers decide their own expressions. Notably, the dataset’s phoneme-balanced scripts were generated using a large language model, making JVNV the first emotional speech corpus to utilize AI-generated content. With a 94% subjective emotion recognition accuracy—surpassing previous Japanese emotional speech datasets—JVNV stands as a valuable resource for speech emotion recognition research.
