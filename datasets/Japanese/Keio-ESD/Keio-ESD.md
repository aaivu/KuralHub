# Keio-ESD (Japanese Emotional Speech Database)  

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** Keio University Japanese Emotional Speech Database (Keio-ESD)  
- **Institution:** Keio University  
- **Year:** 2006  
- **Authors:** Moriyama, T.  
- **Homepage:** [SRC Repository](https://research.nii.ac.jp/src/en/Keio-ESD.html)  

## Dataset Content

- **Duration:** Not specified  
- **Sessions:** Single recording session  
- **Speakers:** 1 male speaker  
- **Sentences:** Multiple Japanese words with 3-4 syllables  
- **Utterances:** Not specified  
- **Language:** Japanese  

## Technical Details

- **Format:** WAV audio files  
- **Audio:** 16 kHz sampling rate  
- **Bit Depth:** 16 bits per sample  
- **Recording:** Professional soundproof room  
- **Data Types:** Human and synthesized audio recordings  
- **Channel:** Mono  

## Emotional Content

- **Primary Emotions:**  
  - Angry  
  - Joyful  
  - Disgusting  
  - Downgrading  
  - Funny  
  - Worried  
  - Gentle  
  - Relief  
  - Indignation  
  - Shameful  
  - (37 additional emotions)  

- **Secondary Emotions:** Total of 47 distinct emotions
