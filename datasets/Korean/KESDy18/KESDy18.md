# KESDy18 (Korean Emotional Speech Dataset)  

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** Korean Emotional Speech Dataset (KESDy18)  
- **Institution:** Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute (ETRI)  
- **Year:** 2018  
- **Authors:** Noh, K.J., et al.  
- **Homepage:** [ETRI AI Data Platform](https://nanum.etri.re.kr/share/kjnoh2/KESDy18?lang=En_us)  

## Dataset Content

- **Duration:** Not specified  
- **Sessions:** Single recording session per speaker  
- **Speakers:** 30 voice actors (15 male, 15 female)  
- **Sentences:** 20 different sentences  
- **Utterances:** 2,248 total recordings  
  - Angry: 431 samples
  - Happy: 157 samples
  - Neutral: 1,193 samples
  - Sad: 467 samples
- **Language:** Korean  

## Technical Details

- **Format:** Audio recordings  
- **Recording Setup:**
  - KESDy18_PM: Galaxy S6 built-in microphone
  - KESDy18_EM: Shure S35 external microphone
- **Recording:** Dual simultaneous recording  
- **Data Types:** Acted speech recordings  
- **Size:** 207.7 MB  

## Emotional Content

- **Primary Emotions:**  
  - Angry  
  - Happy  
  - Neutral  
  - Sad  

- **Annotation Details:**
  - 6 external taggers
  - 7 categorical emotion labels (6 basic + neutral)
  - 5-point scale for arousal and valence
  - Majority voting for final labels
