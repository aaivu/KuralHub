## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
This dataset comprises recordings of three Hungarian sentences, each articulated to express eight fundamental emotions: happiness, sadness, anger, fear, surprise, disgust, contempt, and neutral. 
These recordings were performed by nonprofessional speakers. 
The primary objective of this dataset is to facilitate the examination of human emotion perception and to support the development of automatic emotion recognition systems. 
To achieve this, the dataset was utilized in perception tests comparing human recognition of emotions and in training Hidden Markov Models (HMM) for automatic emotion recognition, optimizing parameters such as acoustic preprocessing and the number of states in the models.
