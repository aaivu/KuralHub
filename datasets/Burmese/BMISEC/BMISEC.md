# BMISEC (Burmese Movies Interviews Speech Emotion Corpus)

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: Burmese Movies Interviews Speech Emotion Corpus (BMISEC)
- **Institution**: University of Computer Studies, Yangon (UCSY)
- **Year**: 2023
- **Authors**: Lwin Lwin Mar, Win Pa Pa, Tin Lay Nwe
- **Homepage**: [UCSY Repository](https://onlineresource.ucsy.edu.mm/handle/123456789/2809)

## Dataset Content
- **Total Files**: 1,332 utterances
- **Total Size**: 2.585 MB
- **Sources**: Natural speech recordings from
  - Myanmar movies
  - Interview recordings
- **Vocalizations**: Natural speech segments
  - Spoken utterances only
  - Duration: 1 hour 56.645 seconds total

## Technical Details
- **Audio Format**: Not specified
- **Video Format**: Source material only (not included)
- **Language**: Burmese (Myanmar)
- **Statements**: Natural speech segments
- **Duration**: Variable length utterances

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Angry
  - Happy
  - Disgust
  - Fear
  - Sad
  - Surprise
  - Neutral
