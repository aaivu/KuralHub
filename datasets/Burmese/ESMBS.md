## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

The **ESMBS (Emotional Speech of Mandarin and Burmese Speakers) dataset** was collected for emotion recognition research. It consists of speech samples from **12 non-professional actors** (six Mandarin and six Burmese speakers, with an equal gender distribution). Each speaker recorded **ten utterances per emotion** across **six emotions**: **anger, disgust, fear, joy, sadness, and surprise**, resulting in a total of **720 emotional utterances**.

Key details:

- **Audio Format**: PCM WAVE, 16-bit, 22,050 Hz
- **Average Sample Length**: 1.50 seconds
- **Emotion Evaluation**: Four listeners, who did not understand the languages, judged the emotional content based on vocal characteristics alone.
- **Human Classification Accuracy**: 65.7% overall (68.3% for Burmese, 63.1% for Mandarin), aligning with previous cross-cultural emotion recognition studies (55%-70% accuracy).
