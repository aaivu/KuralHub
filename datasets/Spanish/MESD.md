## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The Mexican Emotional Speech Database (MESD) contains single-word utterances expressing six emotions (anger, disgust, fear, happiness, neutral, sadness) with Mexican cultural shaping. It includes recordings from 3 female, 2 male, and 6 child non-professional actors, captured in a professional studio at 48kHz, 24-bit WAV format. The dataset features natural and naturalness-reduced versions, with modifications in pitch, formants, and intensity. Words were selected based on linguistic and emotional criteria. MESD is the first dataset of its kind for Mexican Spanish, covering both adult and child voices.
