## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

The Emotional Speech Database (ESD) is a comprehensive resource developed to address the growing research needs in emotional voice conversion. The Mandarin portion of ESD features 350 parallel utterances spoken by 10 native Mandarin speakers expressing 5 distinct emotion categories: neutral, happiness, anger, sadness, and surprise. The recordings were conducted in a controlled acoustic environment to ensure high-quality audio samples.

Part of a larger dataset that also includes English speakers, ESD was specifically designed to facilitate multi-speaker and cross-lingual emotional voice conversion studies. With over 29 hours of speech data in total, this dataset provides researchers with robust material to develop and test emotional voice conversion systems across different languages and emotional expressions.

The parallel nature of the utterances (same content expressed with different emotions) makes it particularly valuable for studying the acoustic characteristics that distinguish various emotional states in speech.
