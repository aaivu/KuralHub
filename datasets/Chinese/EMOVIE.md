# 🎙️ EMOVIE - Mandarin Chinese

🔹 **Dataset Name:** EMOVIE (Mandarin Emotional Movie Speech Dataset) <br/>
🔹 **Language:** Mandarin Chinese <br/>
🔹 **Dataset Type:** 🎙️ Audio / 📄 Text <br/>
🔹 **Emotion Categories:** 5-point polarity scale (Strong Negative to Strong Positive) <br/>
🔹 **Size:** 9,724 utterances, 4.18 hours total <br/>
🔹 **Recording Environment:** 🎬 Movie speech <br/>
🔹 **License:** Not Specified <br/>
🔹 **Access:** 🔗 Open <br/>

---

## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

EMOVIE is a Mandarin Chinese emotional speech dataset containing 9,724 utterances extracted from seven Mandarin movies. The dataset provides both audio and text transcriptions with emotion polarity annotations on a 5-point scale. The audio is extracted from the front center channel of 5.1/7.1 movie audio using ffmpeg, with a sampling rate of 22.05 kHz. The average utterance length is 1.78 seconds with an average of 8.93 characters per utterance.
