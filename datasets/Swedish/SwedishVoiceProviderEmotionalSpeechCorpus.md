## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The Swedish Voice Provider Emotional Speech Corpus comprises recordings from various voice-controlled telephone services operated by the Swedish company Voice Provider. The dataset includes approximately 10,000 utterances, capturing spontaneous speech interactions between users and automated systems. While the dataset is not explicitly labeled for emotions, studies have inferred categories such as Neutral, Frustration, and Uncertainty based on acoustic features. This corpus is valuable for research in emotion recognition within spontaneous speech and human-computer interaction contexts.
