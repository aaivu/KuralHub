# Malay Speech Emotion Dataset

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: Emolah
- **Institution**: Universiti Teknologi MARA, Malaysia
- **Year**: 2018
- **Authors**: 
  - Izzad Ramli
  - Nursuriati Jamil
  - Norizah Ardi
  - Raseeda Hamzah
- **Homepage**: [Paper Link](https://izzadramli.uitm-apps.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Emolah_A_Malay_Language_Spontaneous_Speech_Emotion.pdf)

## Dataset Content
- **Total Duration**: 4 hours and 13 seconds
- **Size**: 450,493 MB
- **Speech Types**:
  - Dialogue recordings (parliament sessions)
  - Interview recordings (talk shows)
  - News readings (radio broadcasts)
- **Speakers**: Age range 6-65 years (specific count not provided)

## Technical Details
- **Language**: Malay
- **Type**: Spontaneous speech recordings
- **Format**: Audio files
- **Sampling Rate**: 16 kHz
- **Channel**: Mono
- **Bit Depth**: 16-bit
- **Utterance Duration**: 3-5 seconds per sample

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Angry (1 hour 4 seconds, ages 6-55)
  - Sad (1 hour 3 seconds, ages 18-65)
  - Happy (1 hour 3 seconds, ages 25-50)
  - Neutral (1 hour 3 seconds, ages 18-50)
