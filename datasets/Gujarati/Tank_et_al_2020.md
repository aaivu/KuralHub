## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The dataset is a Gujarati emotional speech corpus designed for speech emotion recognition (SER). It contains speech samples categorized into six emotional states: sadness, surprise, anger, disgust, fear, and happiness. The dataset has been analyzed using pitch, energy, and MFCC features in MATLAB.
