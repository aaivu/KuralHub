# Serbian Emotional Speech Database (GEES)

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: GEES (Serbian Emotional Speech Database)
- **Institution**: Faculty of Dramatic Arts, Belgrade University
- **Year**: 2004
- **Authors**: Faculty of Dramatic Arts team
- **Homepage**: [ELRA Catalogue](https://catalogue.elra.info/en-us/repository/browse/ELRA-S0385/)

## Dataset Content
- **Total Duration**: Approximately 3 hours
- **Total Recordings**: 2,790
- **Speakers**: 6 actors (3 male, 3 female)
- **Speech Types**:
  - 32 isolated words
  - 30 short sentences (semantically neutral)
  - 30 long sentences (semantically neutral)
  - 1 passage (79 words)

## Technical Details
- **Language**: Serbian
- **Type**: Speech recordings
- **Format**: WAV files with label.txt files
- **Recording Environment**: Anechoic studio
- **Sampling Rate**: 22.05 kHz
- **Original Recording**: DAT tape at 44.1 kHz

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Neutral
  - Anger
  - Happiness
  - Sadness
  - Fear

## Research Impact
- **License**: ELRA END USER (non-commercial) or ELRA VAR (commercial)
- **Applications**: 
  - Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) research
  - Classification studies (HMM: 72%, SVM: 62.78-91.3%)
  - Cross-cultural emotion perception studies
  - Benchmark for Slavic language emotion databases
- **Usage**: 
  - Academic Non-Commercial: Free
  - Commercial Use: 1,500.00 € (ELRA members) / 3,000.00 € (non-members)

## Validation
- **Validation Rate**: 93.33-96.06%
- **Method**: Forced choice listening tests
- **Participants**: 30 normal-hearing students
- **Phonetic Balance**: Verified against Serbian language statistics
