- **Name**: Canadian French Emotional (CaFE) Speech Dataset

- **Institution**: Université de Sherbrooke, Canada
- **Authors**: Gournay, P., Lahaie, O., & Lefebvre, R.

## Dataset Content
- **Total Duration**: About one hour of emotional speech
- **Actors**: 12 total actors
  - 6 male
  - 6 female
- **Sentences**: 6 different sentences in Canadian French
- **Language**: Canadian French

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**: 7 categories
  - Anger
  - Disgust
  - Happiness
  - Fear
  - Surprise
  - Sadness
  - Neutral state

## Technical Details
- **Format**: High-quality audio recordings
- **Recording Environment**: Professional studio quality
- **Structure**: Each actor pronounces all sentences in all emotions
- **Quality**: Clean recordings in controlled environment

## Research Impact
- **Citations**: 46 (as of 2024)
- **Applications**: 
  - Speech Emotion Recognition
  - French Language Processing
  - Cross-lingual Emotion Analysis
  - Emotional Speech Synthesis
