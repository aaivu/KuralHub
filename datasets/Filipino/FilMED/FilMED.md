# Filipino Multimodal Emotion Database (FilMED)

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: FilMED (Filipino Multimodal Emotion Database)
- **Institution**: De La Salle University, Manila
- **Year**: 2013
- **Authors**: 
  - Jocelynn W. Cu
  - Katrina Ysabel C. Solomon
  - Merlin Teodosia C. Suarez
  - Madelene A. Sta. Maria
- **Homepage**: [Research Paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12193-012-0114-8)

## Dataset Content
- **Total Size**: 11,430 audio-video clips
  - FilMED1 (Acted): 10,500 clips (885MB)
    - 7,000 from professional actors
    - 3,500 from volunteers
  - FilMED2 (Spontaneous): 930 clips (1.07GB)
    - 212 audio clips
    - 472 video clips
    - 246 images
- **Speakers**: 25 subjects
- **Speech Types**:
  - Acted expressions (FilMED1)
  - Spontaneous expressions from TV shows (FilMED2)

## Technical Details
- **Language**: Filipino
- **Type**: Multimodal (Audio-Video)
- **Format**: Various multimedia formats
- **Duration Range**:
  - FilMED1: 0.32-5.64 seconds
  - FilMED2: 1-32 seconds
- **Recording Environment**:
  - FilMED1: Controlled room with acoustic padding
  - FilMED2: TV show footage

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Kasiyahan (happiness)
  - Kalungkutan (sadness)
  - Galit (anger)
  - Takot (fear)
  - Gulat (surprise)
  - Pandidiri (disgust)

## Research Impact
- **Applications**: 
  - TALA ambient intelligent space
  - Emotion recognition systems
  - Cross-cultural emotion studies
- **Performance**:
  - Emoticon system: 77.29% accuracy
  - SMERFS system: 80% accuracy
  - SAM-D2: RMSE of 0.4638 (valence), 0.2925 (arousal)
- **Funding**: DOST-PCIEERD and DLSU-URCO

## Validation
- **Annotators**: 20 coders
- **Method**: 
  - Categorical emotion labeling
  - Dimensional annotation (valence/arousal)
- **Tools**: Feeltrace annotation system
