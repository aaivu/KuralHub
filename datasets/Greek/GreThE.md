## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The **GreThE (Greek Theatrical Emotion Dataset)** is a collection of 500 audio samples from 90 unique speakers across 23 Greek theatrical plays. The dataset is annotated for **valence**(negative/neutral/positive) and **arousal** (weak/neutral/strong). It is designed for **Speech Emotion Recognition (SER)** tasks, particularly in the context of theatrical performances. The dataset includes Mel-spectrograms and segment feature vectors for each audio sample, making it suitable for machine learning and deep learning experiments.
