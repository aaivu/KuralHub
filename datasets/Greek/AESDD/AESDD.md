# AESDD Dataset

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: AESDD (Acted Emotional Speech Dynamic Database)
- **Year**: 2018
- **Institution**: M3C Research Group, AUTH

## Dataset Content
- **Size**: ~500 utterances, 0.392 GB
- **Speakers**: Over 5 actors
- **Type**: Professional acted speech

## Technical Details
- **Language**: Greek
- **Type**: Speech recordings
- **Recognition Rate**: ~74% human accuracy

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - anger
  - disgust
  - fear
  - happiness
  - sadness

## Research Impact
- **Applications**: Speech Emotion Recognition
- **Purpose**: SER research and development
