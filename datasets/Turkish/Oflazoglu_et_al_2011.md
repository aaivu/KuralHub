## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The dataset consists of 5304 speech signals from 55 Turkish movies, with their textual contents extracted. These speech signals are labeled both categorically (happy, surprised, sad, angry, fear, neutral, and other) and in a 3-dimensional emotional space (valence, activation, and dominance). It is designed for emotion recognition and acoustic analysis of Turkish emotional speech.
