# 🎙️ EmoTa - Tamil

🔹 **Dataset Name:** EmoTa </br>
🔹 **Language:** Tamil  </br>
🔹 **Dataset Type:** 🎙️ Audio  </br>
🔹 **Emotion Categories:** Happy, Sad, Angry, Fear, Neutral  </br>
🔹 **Size:** Samples: 936, Speakers: 22, duration: 48min  </br>
🔹 **Recording Environment:** 🎭 Acted </br>
🔹 **License:** EmoTa Academic-Commercial </br>
🔹 **Access:** 🔗 Open

---

## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
Collected by the Emota team, this dataset contributes to Tamil Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) focusing on Sri Lankan Tamil. It includes recordings from undergraduates who took Drama Grade B or above at the O/L level, covering diverse regions within Sri Lanka.
