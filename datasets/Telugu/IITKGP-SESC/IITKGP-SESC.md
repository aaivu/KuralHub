# Telugu Speech Emotion Database (IITKGP-SESC)

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: IITKGP-SESC (IIT Kharagpur Simulated Emotion Speech Corpus)
- **Institution**: Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur
- **Year**: 2009
- **Authors**: 
  - Shashidhar G. Koolagudi
  - Sudhamay Maity
  - Vuppala Anil Kumar
  - Saswat Chakrabarti
  - K. Sreenivasa Rao
- **Homepage**: [Research Paper](http://cdn.iiit.ac.in/cdn/speech.iiit.ac.in/svlpubs/conference/anilkumar-ksrao.pdf)

## Dataset Content
- **Total Size**: 12,000 utterances
- **Speakers**: 10 professional artists (5 male, 5 female)
- **Speech Types**: Professional studio recordings

## Technical Details
- **Language**: Telugu
- **Type**: Speech recordings
- **Format**: Audio files (16 kHz, 16-bit)
- **Recording Equipment**: SHURE dynamic cardioid microphone C660N

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Happiness
  - Surprise
  - Anger
  - Disgust
  - Sadness
  - Fear
  - Sarcasm
  - Neutral

## Research Impact
- **Applications**: Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) research
- **Performance**: Up to 95.1% accuracy in recent studies
- **Usage**: Research and benchmarking
