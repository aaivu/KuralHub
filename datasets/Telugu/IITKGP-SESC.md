## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The IITKGP Simulated Emotion Speech Corpus (IITKGP-SESC) is a Telugu speech emotion dataset recorded using professional artists from All India Radio (AIR), Vijayawada, India. It contains emotionally simulated speech covering eight emotions: Happiness, Surprise, Anger, Disgust, Sadness, Fear, Sarcasm, and Neutral, derived from neutral statements. The dataset is designed for emotion analysis in speech, enabling the development of emotion-specific models using features from the vocal tract system, excitation source, and prosody. The quality of emotions is validated through subjective listening tests, and statistical models are used for classification.
