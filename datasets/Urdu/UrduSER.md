## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The Urdu Speech Emotion Corpus (UrSEC) is a comprehensive dataset for Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) in Urdu, addressing the lack of large and diverse datasets in this language. It contains 3500 speech samples from 10 professional actors, representing seven emotional states: Angry, Fear, Boredom, Disgust, Happy, Neutral, and Sad. The samples are sourced from Pakistani Urdu drama serials and telefilms, ensuring natural and diverse speech. Each emotional class has 500 samples, with detailed metadata for easy organization and use in research. UrSEC is rigorously validated for accuracy and reliability, supporting advancements in Urdu SER.
