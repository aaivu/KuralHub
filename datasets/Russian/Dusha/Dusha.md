# Dusha (Russian Emotional Speech Dataset)  

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** Dusha  
- **Institution:** Salute AI Team  
- **Year:** 2023  
- **Authors:** Salute Developers  
- **Homepage:** [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xbgoose/dusha)  

## Dataset Content

- **Duration:** ~350 hours total  
- **Sessions:** Multiple recording sessions  
- **Speakers:** Not specified  
- **Utterances:** 164,387 total recordings  
- **Audio Length:** 0.06s to 24.1s per sample  
- **Language:** Russian  

## Technical Details

- **Format:** Audio recordings with transcripts  
- **Size:** 
  - Hugging Face: 21.5 GB
  - Kaggle: 30.32 GB
- **Structure:**
  - 18 columns
  - 202k files
  - Two folders: crowd_test and crowd_train
- **Data Types:** Bi-modal (audio and text)  

## Emotional Content

- **Primary Emotions:**  
  - Happiness (Positive)  
  - Sadness  
  - Anger  
  - Neutral  

- **Secondary Emotions:** None specified  

## Research Impact

- **Citations:** Used in speech emotion recognition studies  
- **Models:** Pre-trained HuBERT and WavLM models available  
- **Publication:** Available through Hugging Face and Kaggle  
- **Access:** Open access
