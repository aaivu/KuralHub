# Marathi Emotional Speech Database

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: Marathi Emotional Speech Database
- **Institution**: EXTC Department, K.J. Somaiya College of Engineering, University of Mumbai
- **Year**: Not specified
- **Authors**: 
  - Dipti D. Joshi
  - M.B. Zalte
- **Homepage**: [Research Paper](http://www.irdindia.in/journal_ijacect/pdf/vol2_iss2/11.pdf)

## Dataset Content
- **Total Size**: 300 recordings
  - 10 speakers × 10 sentences × 3 repetitions
- **Speakers**: 10 total
  - 5 male speakers
  - 5 female speakers
- **Speech Types**:
  - 10 sentences per speaker
  - Each sentence repeated 3 times
  - Continuous sentences in Marathi

## Technical Details
- **Language**: Marathi
- **Type**: Speech recordings
- **Recording Equipment**:
  - Electrat microphone
  - Distance: 30 cm from speaker's mouth
- **Recording Environment**: 
  - Partially sound treated room
- **Recording Specifications**:
  - Software: PRAAT
  - Sampling rate: 16 kHz
  - Bit depth: 16-bit

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Happiness
  - Anger
  - Neutral
  - Fear
  - Sadness
  - Boredom

## Research Impact
- **Applications**: 
  - Speech Emotion Recognition (SER)
  - Emotional prosody analysis
  - Cross-lingual studies
- **Validation**:
  - 10 naive listeners (age 18-28 years)
  - 80% agreement threshold
  - Educated evaluators
