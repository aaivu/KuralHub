## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
This database includes 7 emotions (Anger, Happiness, Sad, Fear, Boredom, Surprise, and Neutral) acted by 6 male and 6 female actors. 
Each actor utters 10 Kurdish sentences on 4 different sessions for each emotion. There is a total of 3360 recordings (i.e.,12×7×10×4 recordings). 
The speakers have been told to act these sentences and express them in their own style, by remembering/imagining a situation with the relevant emotion. 
The speakers have different acting experience (2 to 8 Years) and ages (19 to 36 years). 
The recording process has been done in a quiet room without restriction in the recording path. 
10 listeners participated in a subjective test to determine the perceptibility of the emotions. The average of correct labeling was 41%.
