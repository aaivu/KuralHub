# Odia Speech Emotion Database (SITB-OSED)

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: SITB-OSED
- **Type**: First open-source Odia speech emotion database
- **Institution**: Silicon Institute of Technology, Bhubaneswar
- **Year**: 2022
- **Authors**: 
  - Bubai Maji (IIT Kharagpur)
  - Monorama Swain
- **Publication**: IEEE CSDE 2022
- **DOI**: 10.1109/CSDE56538.2022.10089254

## Dataset Content
- **Total Utterances**: 12,110
- **Speakers**: 20 professional Odia native speakers
  - 10 male speakers
  - 10 female speakers
  - 4 speakers per dialect (2 male, 2 female)
- **Speech Types**:
  - Five major Odia dialects:
    - Cuttacki
    - Baleswari
    - Berhampuri
    - Sambalpuri
    - Phulbani

## Technical Details
- **Language**: Odia
- **Format**: WAV files
- **Channels**: 2 (stereo)
- **Bit Depth**: 16-bit
- **Sample Rate**: 22.05 kHz
- **Duration**: 3.5s to 8s per utterance
- **Recording Environment**: Quiet environment

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Anger
  - Surprise
  - Happy
  - Sadness
  - Disgust
  - Fear

## Research Impact
- **Significance**: First open-source large speech emotional database in Odia
- **Applications**: 
  - Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) research
  - Academic research
  - Practical applications
- **Coverage**: Represents all major Odia dialects
- **Validation**: Tested with multiple SER baseline systems
