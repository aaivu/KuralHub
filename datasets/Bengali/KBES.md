## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
The KUET Bangla Emotional Speech (KBES) dataset is a Bangla speech emotion recognition (SER) dataset containing 900 speech samples from 35 actors (20 females and 15 males) across diverse age ranges. The dataset includes five emotional categories: Neutral, Happy, Sad, Angry, and Disgust. Except for Neutral, each emotion is further divided into two intensity levels: Low and High, resulting in a total of nine classes. The speech samples are sourced from Bangla Telefilms, Dramas, TV Series, and Web Series, making the dataset more natural and expressive. Each class contains 100 gender-balanced samples, ensuring data symmetry for effective emotion classification.
