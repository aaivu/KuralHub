# EMO-DB (Berlin Database of Emotional Speech)

## Dataset Overview

- **Name:** Berlin Database of Emotional Speech (EMO-DB)  
- **Institution:** Technical University Berlin  
- **Year:** 2005  
- **Authors:** Burkhardt, F., Paeschke, A., Rolfes, M., Sendlmeier, W., & Weiss, B.  
- **Homepage:** [EMO-DB Dataset](http://emodb.bilderbar.info/index-1280.html)  

## Dataset Content

- **Duration:** ~800 utterances  
- **Sessions:** Multiple recording sessions  
- **Speakers:** 10 actors (5 male, 5 female)  
- **Sentences:** 10 German utterances (5 short, 5 longer)  
- **Utterances:** ~800 total recordings  
- **Language:** German  

## Technical Details

- **Format:** High-quality audio  
- **Audio:** 16 kHz sampling rate (downsampled from 48 kHz)  
- **Recording:** Anechoic chamber  
- **Equipment:** Sennheiser MKH 40 P48 microphone  
- **Data Types:** Audio recordings, electro-glottograms  

## Emotional Content

- **Primary Emotions:**
  - Anger  
  - Boredom  
  - Disgust  
  - Fear  
  - Happiness  
  - Neutral  
  - Sadness  

- **Secondary Emotions:** None specified
