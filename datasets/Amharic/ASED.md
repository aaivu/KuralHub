# ASED (Amharic Speech Emotion Dataset)

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: Amharic Speech Emotion Dataset (ASED)
- **Institution**: Northwest University
- **Year**: 2022
- **Authors**: Ephrem Afele Retta, Eiad Almekhlafi, Richard Sutcliffe, Mustafa Mhamed, Haider Ali, Jun Feng
- **Homepage**: [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Ethio2021/ASED_V1)

## Dataset Content
- **Total Files**: 2,474 sound samples
- **Total Size**: Not specified
- **Speakers**: 65 volunteers
  - 25 female
  - 40 male
- **Vocalizations**: 27 sentences per speaker
  - 25 emotion-specific sentences
  - 2 common sentences

## Technical Details
- **Audio Format**: WAV (16-bit, 16kHz)
- **Video Format**: Not available
- **Language**: Amharic
- **Dialects**: Four regional variants
  - Gojjam
  - Wollo
  - Shewa
  - Gonder
- **Duration**: 2-4 seconds per file

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Neutral
  - Fearful
  - Happy
  - Sad
  - Angry

## Expression Intensity
- **Intensity Levels**: Not specified

## Research Impact
- **Published**: ACM Transactions on Asian and Low-Resource Language Information Processing (2022)
- **DOI**: 10.1145/3529759
- **Application**: Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) research
- **Validation**: Independently validated by 8 judges
