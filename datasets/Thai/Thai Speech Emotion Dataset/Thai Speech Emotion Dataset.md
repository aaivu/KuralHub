# Thai Speech Emotion Dataset

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: Thai Speech Emotion Dataset
- **Institution**: AI Research Thailand (AIAT)
- **Year**: Released publicly
- **Authors**: AI Research Thailand
- **Homepage**: [AIAT Repository](https://airesearch.in.th/releases/speech-emotion-dataset/)

## Dataset Content
- **Total Duration**: 36 hours
- **Speakers**: 200 performers (male and female)
- **Speech Types**:
  - Monologue recordings
  - Dialogue recordings

## Technical Details
- **Language**: Thai
- **Type**: Speech recordings
- **Format**: Audio files

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Anger
  - Sadness
  - Frustration
  - Happiness
  - Standard tones
