## 📜 **Dataset Overview**
IEMOCAP is a multimodal, multispeaker dataset containing ~12 hours of audiovisual data, including speech, video, facial motion capture, and text transcriptions. It features dyadic sessions with scripted and improvised interactions designed to elicit emotions. The dataset is annotated with categorical (anger, happiness, sadness, neutrality) and dimensional (valence, activation, dominance) labels, making it valuable for studying expressive human communication.
