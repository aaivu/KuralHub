## 📜 **Dataset Overview**

This dataset is part of research focused on emotion recognition from speech using artificial neural networks. It includes gender-dependent databases and utilizes features like discrete wavelet transforms, low-pass filters, and frequency filtering to extract speech features for emotion classification. The dataset is part of an effort to advance affective computing and automatic emotion recognition.
