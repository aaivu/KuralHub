# Estonian Emotional Speech Corpus (EEKK)

## Dataset Overview
- **Name**: Estonian Emotional Speech Corpus (EEKK)
- **Institution**: Institute of the Estonian Language
- **Year**: 2006
- **Authors**: National Programme for Estonian Language Technology
- **Homepage**: [META-SHARE Repository](https://metashare.ut.ee/repository/browse/estonian-emotional-speech-corpus/)

## Dataset Content
- **Total Size**: 339 MB
- **Total Recordings**: 1,234 sentences
- **Speakers**: Single female voice
- **Speech Types**:
  - Read speech sentences
  - Text passages with emotional content

## Technical Details
- **Language**: Estonian
- **Type**: Speech recordings
- **Format**: Mono WAV files
- **Sampling Rate**: 44.1 kHz
- **Bit Depth**: 16-bit

## Emotional Content
- **Primary Emotions**:
  - Anger
  - Joy
  - Sadness
  - Neutral

## Research Impact
- **License**: Available through META-SHARE repository
- **Applications**: 
  - Text-to-speech synthesis
  - Emotion recognition research
  - Cross-cultural studies
- **Usage**: Research and academic purposes
